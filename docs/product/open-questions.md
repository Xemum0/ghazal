# Ghazal — Open questions for the owner

> The registry of every decision the design or the build cannot make alone. `AGENTS.md`: *when the PRD is ambiguous, pick the most defensible option, record it here, keep moving.* Every row therefore has a **working assumption** that the design already uses, so nothing is blocked on an answer; an answer changes configuration, copy or a single path, not the architecture.
> IDs are permanent (ADRs and screens cite them). **OQ-B** booking, money and operations · **OQ-L** localisation, content and analytics · **OQ-T** technical and delivery · **OQ-P** product scope (added from the research files and the screen inventory) · **OQ-V** voucher marketplace model (section 2b, added with ADR 0033). NOW in fixtures = Thu 12 Nov 2026.

**Status values.** `Open` = no owner answer yet, design proceeds on the working assumption. `Answered` = moved to section 6 with date and ADR. `Superseded` = dissolved by another answer.
**Needed by** (last token in each Status cell): **W0** Week 0, before the first commit under `apps/`, any account or bundle ID · **DF** design freeze, end of Week 1, tag `design-v1` · **BUILD** before the build week of the feature it gates · **LAUNCH** before store submission.
Where two rows ask the same thing, answer once (section 7).
