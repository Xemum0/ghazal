01 · Concept

Every venue is a doorway.

A couple is not buying square metres. She is deciding which room she will walk into on the night of her life. The single idea in Majlis is the arch crown: a pointed, slightly horseshoe silhouette that frames every photograph that matters, crowns every sheet that asks for a decision, and relaxes to a straight lintel when a sheet is pulled to full height — the door opens.

The one memorable idea

One silhouette, three places, never decoration.

  • Hero crown clips the home photograph (rise 64).
  • Sheet crown tops every sheet and the venue panel (rise 24). Dragged to full height it flattens to 0 over 320 ms.
  • Compare doors frame the three venues side by side (rise 20, docked 12).

1

Ink is the wall, ivory is the room.

Large Ink surfaces mark the moments of decision: the hero, the summary bars, the tab bar, the call to action. Content lives on warm ivory with hairline rules, never on stacks of shadowed cards.

2

Gold is structure.

A 2 px lintel above each section, hairline plinths under featured photos, the knots on a progress thread, and the one primary action per screen. Gold text is always Gold-ink 700; Gold 500 is never a text colour.

3

Lattice sits in the spandrels.

The geometric screen appears only where architecture would put it: around the doorway in the hero (and in empty states). Gold at 20 % on Ink, 1 px stroke, 1.1 % of the home fold’s pixels (measured). A CustomPainter, not an asset.

4

Brochure columns, not card piles.

Content snaps to 2, 3 or 4 equal columns separated by 1 px rules, like a printed hotel brochure. Elevation is reserved for the one floating object per screen.

The brochure grid fluid from 360 to 430

  • Measure is the frame minus two 20 px gutters: 320 at 360, 350 at 390, 390 at 430.
  • Columns are equal thirds or quarters divided by 1 px rules (compare, facts, categories). Never gaps plus shadows.
  • Hero bleeds: images run edge to edge; text re-enters at the gutter.
  • Safe areas: 47 top, 34 bottom, handled by the bars, not by content.
  • Everything in logical order: inline-start / inline-end, so the same layout mirrors in RTL with no second stylesheet.
05 · Icons

Drawn like a floor plan.

One set on a 24 px grid with a 2 px keyline margin: 1.5 px stroke, square caps, mitre joins, no rounded ends. Corners are the geometry of arches and diamonds, not soft curves. Strokes are compensated at every size so the rendered line is always 1.5 px. Only directional glyphs mirror in RTL.

11 · Honest risks

Where this could go wrong.

The arch can become a gimmick

It works because it is rationed to three places. Every new surface will want an arch; the rule is that nothing else gets one. If research says couples read it as “mosque”, the crown rise can drop to 12 and the idea survives as a flat lintel.

Ink-heavy chrome costs photo space

The Ink tab bar (94 px) and CTA bar (about 120 px) cost 11–14 % of an 844 px screen. They earn it by making the primary action unmissable, but a long browse screen may want the bar to hide on scroll.

Markazi runs small

It is sized +22 % to match Newsreader and still reads lighter than Cairo beside it. Fallback is Amiri or Noto Naskh; the scale tokens already carry per-script sizes so swapping is one token.

The hotel app is deliberately plain

No arch, no lattice, one display line. Owners will love the calm; a brand review may find it generic. The gold thread as an SLA clock is the one signature we spend there.

Flutter, token to widget

  • ThemeExtension holds the ramps, spacing, radius, motion and the two rise values.
  • CrownClipper (CustomClipper<Path>) and CrownBorder (ShapeBorder with a Gold stroke) from the two cubics above.
  • LatticePainter draws one 56 dp octagram tile (tip .40 T, valley .306 T, 1 dp stroke) clipped to the spandrel path.
  • ThreadProgress is a CustomPainter: one 2 dp line, circular knots, direction from Directionality.
  • Icons ship as an SVG sprite compiled to a custom icon font; mirrored via matchTextDirection.

What is out of scope here

  • Dark mode: Ink-on-ivory inverts naturally, but it is not designed in this pass.
  • Booking steps, payments, chat, planner: the sheet, thread and row patterns above are meant to carry them.
  • Web admin: the hotel pattern (ledger rows, gold rule) extends to it directly.
