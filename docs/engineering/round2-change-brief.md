# Round 2 change brief (2026-10-29, ADR 0038)

> Standing brief for the agents that apply the owner's round 2 decisions to the design, the contract and mock, and both Flutter apps. The decisions are in `docs/decisions/0038-owner-decisions-round-2.md` (binding); requirement IDs `CA-AUTH-13`, `CA-AUTH-14`, `CA-ONB-10`, `CA-FLT-08`, `CA-SVC-19`, `CA-SVC-20`, `PA-ACC-07`, `PA-ACC-08` in `docs/product/feature-matrix.json`; working assumptions OQ-R2-01..04 in `docs/product/open-questions.md`. Everything else in the product is unchanged. Never invent scope: a doubt goes to `docs/product/open-questions.md` (append with `>>`) with a working assumption.

## The five changes, as end states

1. **Governorates, not areas (CA-FLT-08, CA-ONB-10).** The Client app has no exact-area selection anywhere. `CL-ONBD-06` (Preferred areas) is retired (inventory `kind: deprecated`, like the five of ADR 0033; keep the id). The Brief sheet (`CL-DISC-02`), Filters (`CL-DISC-04`), Results (`CL-DISC-03`: the "All areas" chip reads "All governorates") and the Vendors filters (`CL-SERV-02`) use governorate chips (multi-select, wrapping chips, a count in the title, glyph plus label, no long text). The governorate list comes from configuration (`GET /areas` already returns `governorates`; start with Kuwait's six official ones, Capital, Hawalli, Farwaniya, Ahmadi, Jahra, Mubarak Al-Kabeer). A venue keeps its area only as an address line. The onboarding length shrinks by one step; the live "N halls match" count (if shown) follows the governorates.
2. **Services independent of venues (CA-SVC-19).** Nothing on the venue side offers or links other services: remove extras and add-on services from hotel and hall packages (client package sheet `CL-VENU-04`, and the Partner app's venue management: the Extras tab/catalogue under `HL-VENU-*`), any service suggestion rail or link on venue pages, and the governmental-facility "Find catering and decor" link. `CL-SERV-01..04` stay as the only way to add services: the client picks a provider and requests a quote from it. The Services hub copy no longer claims to be "where the governmental-facility link lands". Do not delete the API fields (additive-only contract): the apps stop showing and editing them.
3. **Partner application (PA-ACC-07, PA-ACC-08).** `HL-PROV-01` (Partner app) becomes the application form of a service provider or a private hall: account type (provider or private hall; a hotel or hall chain is onboarded as today), business name in Arabic and English, categories (up to three from the registered list) plus an open "Other / not in the list" note, governorate, contact person, phone, email, short description, optional commercial licence reference (OQ-R2-03). Submit shows "Application received" and the account stays under review; Ghazal staff accept or reject (reason shown on rejection, with "Edit and resubmit"); only an accepted account can create listings. `HL-ACCS-04` (account status) gains the two states: application under review, application rejected with the reason. Staff decisions happen in the Super Admin web (out of scope): the mock exposes a control endpoint for it. No form in the Client app.
4. **Client note for service providers (CA-SVC-20).** One small note, glyph plus one line, "Service provider? Apply in the Partner app" (AR «هل أنت مزوّد خدمة؟ قدّم طلبك في تطبيق الشريك»), in the Services hub (`CL-SERV-01`, footer) and the Profile hub (`CL-PROF-01`, a row), pointing to the Partner app store page (URL from configuration, OQ-R2-04). Text diet: no paragraph.
5. **No guest mode (CA-AUTH-13, CA-AUTH-14).** Client app first run: Language (`CL-ONBD-01`), Introduction (`CL-ONBD-02`, skip goes to sign-in, not Home), Sign in (`CL-AUTH-01`, now a full screen, phone number first; no "continue as guest", no guest-gate sheet variant), code (`CL-AUTH-02`), About you (`CL-AUTH-03`: name required and sex required, female or male as two large choices with glyphs, then the Terms and Privacy acceptance), then the optional onboarding steps (`CL-ONBD-03` role and names prefilled from the account, `CL-ONBD-04` date, `CL-ONBD-05` guests and budget; all skippable), then Home. Returning users sign in and land on Home. A signed-out client never sees a venue, a price or a screen behind the sign-in. Every guest state of the Client screens is removed (venue detail "works fully for guests", the gate before Get voucher and Send request, signed-out Saved, Messages, My Wedding, Profile). Name and sex are editable in `CL-PROF-03` (Personal info). The Partner app keeps its signed-out state (`HL-ACCS-*`).

## Round 2b (2026-10-29, owner feedback; OQ-R2-11 resolved, OQ-R2-13)

1. **Findable application.** `HL-ACCS-01` (Partner sign in) gets a visible secondary button "Apply to join" («قدّم طلب انضمام»). It opens `HL-PROV-01` signed out. The form starts with an "Your account" section (email, password, repeat password), then the business information. Submit creates the account and the pending application, then `HL-ACCS-02` verifies the email with a code (existing screen), then `HL-ACCS-04` shows "application under review" (existing). A rejected applicant signs in later with the same email and password and sees the reason and "Edit and resubmit" (existing). Contract: new public operation `submitPartnerApplication` (no session; account fields + the existing application fields; answers the verification challenge like partner sign-in does); everything signed-in stays as built.
2. **Visible provider note.** The Client app note for service providers is one visible line of text («هل أنت مزوّد خدمة؟ قدّم طلبك في تطبيق الشريك» / "Service provider? Apply in the Partner app") with a clear call to action (a trailing "Apply" label or chevron and the accent colour), full width, 44 dp, in the Services hub footer and as a row in the Profile hub. No info glyph hiding the words.
