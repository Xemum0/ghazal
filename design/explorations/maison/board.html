Maison

Editorial hospitality

Maison is the feeling of being expected. Ghazal behaves like the front desk of a house you would be glad to be a guest in: photography is the architecture, type is the signage, and one gold thread, the khayt, runs through everything that matters.

«دار»: شعورٌ بأنّ أحداً بانتظاركم. غزل يتصرّف كمكتب الاستقبال في بيتٍ يسرّكم أن تكونوا ضيوفه: الصورة هي العمارة، والخطّ هو اللافتات، وخيطٌ ذهبيٌّ واحد يمرّ بكلّ ما يهمّ.

01

The one memorable idea · الفكرة

The brief hints that غزل means both love poem and spun thread. Here that meaning does work instead of decorating.

One hairline, six jobs.

The khayt (خيط, thread) is a single 1 px gold line with a diamond knot. It is never ornament: wherever it appears it states a fact about the page.

Why it earns its place. The thread is the product's promise made visible (certainty about the date, the deposit and the reply) and it needs no illustration, no pattern and no extra colour. It costs one hairline and an 8 px diamond, it mirrors for free with logical properties, and it is the only element allowed to animate by itself, once, on arrival.

غزلُ الشعر وغزلُ الخيط: خطٌّ ذهبيٌّ رفيع واحد، يُخبرنا أين نحن في الحجز، وكم بقي من الوقت، وماذا اخترنا.

The gold problem, solved

Matte gold on warm off-white is 1.91 : 1, so gold is only ever a fill, a hairline or a knot. Anything that must be read in gold uses gold-700 (5.96 : 1). Labels on gold fills use ink-900 (7.45 : 1); core ink on gold (4.12) is deliberately not used for text.

Where this direction could be wrong.

  1. Serif display in a data-heavy hotel app. Newsreader on dates and totals reads as editorial, which suits Khalid at 08:30 but may slow Maryam at a desk; if testing says so, drop the serif from the triage card and keep it for venue names only.
  2. Two figure voices. Serif above 20 px, Cairo below, is a rule designers must hold; a Flutter text-style helper has to enforce it.
  3. The thread depends on restraint. If every screen adds a second gold element it stops being a signal. Proposed lint: gold fills only for the selected day, count badges and knots; never a button, panel or banner.
  4. Photography carries the colour. The look relies on well-graded, empty-room images; a weak library makes the screens flat. Rose and gold cannot rescue a bad photograph.
  5. The brief as a sentence. Memorable and calm, but less scannable than labelled fields and the underlined words must read as controls on first use. If testing says otherwise, fall back to the four-field grid (the .brief__cell pattern is kept in direction.css).
  6. Amiri ligatures. The lam-khah join in الخليج is classical and legible but may be unfamiliar to younger readers; to be checked in the Arabic usability session.
  7. Contrast on photography. White type over the cover scrim is single-purpose and subtle; bright beach photographs need a per-image scrim check at upload.
