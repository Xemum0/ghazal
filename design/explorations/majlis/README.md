# Ghazal · Majlis — Architectural warmth

**Concept.** A booking is a threshold. Gulf hospitality architecture, withheld to three devices: the **arch crown** (the one memorable idea: a two-Bézier silhouette that frames hero photos, crowns sheets, and flattens to a lintel at full height), a **lattice** kept in spandrels and empty states (1.1 % of the fold's pixels, a CustomPainter), and **gold as line work**. The hotel app is the same building's back-of-house: no arch, a gold rule, a gold SLA thread.

**Colour roles.** Ink is the wall (hero, tab and CTA bars, 13 % of pixels), ivory the room (72 %), Soft Rose quiet panels (9 %), Matte Gold fills and hairlines (2 %). Gold text uses Gold-ink `#7A5A1C` (6.08:1); Gold 500 is never text.

**Type.** Poppins and Cairo carry all UI; Cairo digits are the tabular "figures" voice. One display pair, Newsreader and Markazi Text, for names and hero lines. Ratio 1.25, Arabic +1 to +4 px and 1.65 leading.

**Shape.** Radii 4 / 8 / 12 / 16, hairline rules, one warm shadow family for the single floating object; all on a 4-pt grid.

**Motion.** 120 / 160 / 240 ms snappy, 320 ms emphasised for sheets, 480 for success.

**Memorable.** Dragging the sheet and watching the crown flatten (board demo B).

**Honest risks.** The arch may be read as "mosque" or turn into a gimmick; Ink chrome costs 11–14 % of the screen; Markazi reads small; the hotel app is deliberately plain; fonts load from Google.
