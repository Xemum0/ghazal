# Ghazal · Maison — editorial hospitality

**Concept.** Ghazal behaves like the front desk of a house you would be glad to be a guest in. Photography is the architecture, serif type is the signage. One gold hairline, the *khayt* (thread, غزل's other meaning), carries facts: section rules, the selected tab, price ranges, time left on a request, booking progress, and the underlined words of the search sentence. Six jobs, no ornament.

**Colour.** Ink `#5A4B4F` carries text and the single primary action. Matte Gold `#D8B26E` is only a fill, hairline or knot (1.91:1 on canvas, never text); `gold-700` `#7A5C1E` is the text/icon gold (5.96:1). Soft Rose is a quiet panel. Canvas `#FFF9F5`. Success and danger are status-only, always icon plus label.

**Type.** Newsreader and Amiri for names, prices and headlines, matched by measured x-height and digit height so EN and AR share a baseline. Poppins and Cairo for UI; Cairo digits are the figures voice. Scale 1.25 on 16; Arabic is ×1.08–1.10 with explicit leading and tightened display word space.

**Shape.** Radii 0/4/8/12. Hairlines before shadows; one warm shadow for floating things. 4-pt grid, 24 gutter, controls 44/52/56.

**Motion.** 120/200/320/480 ms, two curves: image reveals, thread draw, one knot pop. Nothing loops; reduced-motion respected.

**Memorable.** The search is a typeset sentence whose underlined words are the controls.

**Honest risks.** Serif in a data-dense hotel app. Sentence discoverability (fallback grid kept). Dependence on empty-room photography. Amiri ligatures for younger readers. Google Fonts availability.
